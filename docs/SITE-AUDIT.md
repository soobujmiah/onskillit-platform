The current site also contains poor SEO signals: demo text, off-domain links and questionable claims. The [public homepage-link inventory](current-site-link-inventory.csv) found 251 anchor occurrences, 111 unique targets, 85 unique same-domain URLs after fragment removal, and 25 unique external/other targets. On 2026-09-24, direct HTTP HEAD requests returned 200 for the homepage and 404 for the other 84 same-domain URLs. Three sampled GET requests (`/about-us/`, `/contact/`, a WordPress course) also returned 404, so this is not merely a HEAD-only behavior in those samples. The indexed gambling URL also returned 404 directly during this check, while a search engine still held its article text, so index cleanup and historical review are required.

Before any redirect or content migration, obtain a read-only CMS export, URL inventory, Search Console indexed URLs, sitemap, analytics landing pages, and relevant publication/access logs. Record URL, status, canonical, title, H1, content hash, links, author/date, owner-approved classification and keep/rewrite/redirect/410 disposition. Preserve evidence of unrelated content and have the site owner review CMS accounts/revisions and security logs. Do not remove pages or alter production in this phase.
