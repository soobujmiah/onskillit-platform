The [NSDA training-institution guidance](https://nsda.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/6922dd02933eb65569e13246) and [NSDA competency standards](https://nsda.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/69687e401fa8288efab67a26) show that formal training recognition has its own processes. This does **not** establish that OnSkillIT is registered/accredited or that a particular course requires a specific approval; the owner and qualified local adviser must review the intended claims and activities. Generic completion certificates must not imply government accreditation. Rights evidence may be stored privately; the public repository records only approval state and non-sensitive provenance.

No policy page may be marked approved merely because the PAGE-PRIVACY or PAGE-TERMS template exists. The relevant Phase 0 legal gate remains **BLOCKED**.
