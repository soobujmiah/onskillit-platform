## Recommended provisional launch set (owner-directed proposal)

To avoid a hollow catalog, prepare a small initial set for partner review: **Office productivity**, **Web development foundations**, **Digital marketing/SEO**, **Digital teaching practice**, **Business website delivery/maintenance**, and **Corporate digital-literacy workshops**. This is a recommendation based on the project direction and Bangladesh skills context, **not** a statement that OnSkillIT currently delivers them. Android app development, UI/UX, cybersecurity, IT support, analytics service and consultancy remain candidates contingent on named expert capacity and real materials. A large public catalog should not launch until each item passes the evidence checklist below.

For every proposed V1 learning offer, require a complete Bangla and English title/description, measurable outcomes, prerequisites, lesson plan, modality and location/timezone, instructor identity/consent, practice work, assessment rubric, accessibility plan, support expectations, BDT price/tax/refund disclosure and truthful certificate wording. For services, require scope, deliverables, client responsibilities, response/support terms, quote method, data-access boundary and a real capability proof. Any claim of NSDA/BTEB accreditation needs separate documentary verification. This quality bar is what makes the proposed catalog suitable for actual operations rather than theme-card decoration.

## Minimum evidence to move a row to VERIFIED

A founding partner identifies offer owner, approved name in both languages, actual service scope or syllabus/outcomes, eligible audience, online/offline/hybrid method, delivery capacity/instructor, fee/tax/refund status, launch availability, media/copy rights, and a dated source document or signed approval. For services, add SLA/support boundary and one credible capability proof; for courses/programs, add edition, prerequisites, assessment/certificate policy and schedule or on-demand status. Record evidence privately if it contains commercial or personal details; the public repo gets only non-sensitive approval metadata. Any old-site card without this review remains unpublished.
